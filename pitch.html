The demo

A 5 mg vs 10 mg mix-up: caught, confirmed, explained

  1. 1

    Lily shares Mom's papers + new bottle. Capy Reader extracts every medication with SIE.

  2. 2

    Cognee flags a conflict: lisinopril 5 mg (Nov 8) vs 10 mg (Nov 11).

  3. 3

    Both doses are normal on the label, so the Guardian asks Lily to call the pharmacy before the next dose.

  4. 4

    "Dr. Alvarez increased it on Nov 10." resolve_conflict stores the family's decision for good.

  5. 5

    Sam: "dizzy since Tuesday." Temporal recall: that's the day after the dose increase. The label lists dizziness, so it goes into the visit brief with 3 questions for the doctor.

Why it matters

  • A real job families do at the kitchen table, done in minutes, with every claim cited.
  • Memory that understands time and contradictions, not just similar text.
  • Small SIE models do the reading; the LLM is used only where it adds judgment.
  • Safe by design: it flags and prepares, and a person decides.
